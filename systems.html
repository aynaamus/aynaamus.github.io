Intelligence systems

Software for investigating complex datasets

Five systems for working through large datasets. Each ingests its data with recorded provenance, keeps its reasoning inspectable, and reports measured results with their limitations.

Engineering is not detection. These pages report what each system does and how it was tested separately from how well its detection rules performed. TRIS, ATIS and BTGI were evaluated on held-out data, and their detection performance is weak. Their alerts are anomalies that call for review. They are not findings of fraud, compromise or illicit activity.

CRIE

Corporate Relationship Intelligence Engine

Investigation workbench for reported corporate consolidation relationships, with sourced claims, append-only reviews and versioned, hash-verified reports.

Transaction monitoring

TRIS

Transaction Risk Intelligence System

SQL-driven monitoring and investigation workbench on 6.36 million synthetic PaySim mobile-money transactions.

Held-out recall 22.4%, precision 16.4% (simulated labels).

Python · DuckDB · NetworkX · Streamlit · Public source

Authentication anomalies

ATIS

Account Takeover Intelligence System

Explainable anomaly rules and relationship analysis over 31.3 million synthesized login events.

Detected 0 of 65 held-out labeled takeover events.

Python · DuckDB · Streamlit · Public source

Blockchain graph analysis

BTGI

Bitcoin Transaction Graph Investigator

Bounded directed-graph exploration of the Elliptic data set, with structural alerts and evidence traced to edges.

Held-out precision 1.15%, below the 6.5% base rate.
