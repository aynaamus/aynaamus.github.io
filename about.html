About

Ayna Musayeva

Investigative Researcher & Software Developer

Ayna Musayeva works on corporate intelligence, financial investigations, open-source intelligence and graph analytics, and builds the software those investigations need.

The research examines concealed ownership structures, complex financing arrangements and regulatory gaps, most extensively in the global art market. It includes clause-level analysis of securities, art-fund and cultural-property frameworks, and close checking of widely cited market statistics against their sources.

The same discipline carries into the software. Systems built in Python on MySQL and DuckDB keep provenance for every input, separate evidence from conclusions, record investigative decisions in append-only audit trails, and report measured performance alongside its limitations.

Areas of work

Investigation

Corporate relationships and ownership, financial networks, regulatory and legal analysis, primary-source verification, OSINT.

Engineering

Python, SQL (MySQL, DuckDB), graph analysis (NetworkX), Streamlit, pytest, retrieval-augmented generation (FAISS, BM25, local LLMs).

Education

MFA, New York Academy of Art (2018). BA, Western Caspian University (2016). Documentary Practices and Visual Journalism, International Center of Photography (2022).
