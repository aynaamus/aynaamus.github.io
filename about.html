About

Ayna Musayeva

Investigative Researcher & Software Developer

Investigative researcher and software developer focused on corporate intelligence, financial investigations, open-source intelligence and graph analytics.

Research covers ownership structures, financing arrangements and regulatory gaps, including clause-level analysis of securities, investment-fund and cultural-property frameworks.

Engineering work includes five purpose-built intelligence systems: corporate ownership mapping (CRIE), transaction monitoring (TRIS), account-takeover investigation (ATIS), blockchain graph analysis (BTGI) and source-verified AI research (Nimillux). All are built in Python, with documented testing and measured results reported alongside their limitations.

Engineering
