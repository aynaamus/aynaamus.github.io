Nimillux — AI Research Infrastructure

A fully local retrieval-augmented research system built on one principle: a generated answer is shown only after each of its claims has been checked against the source passages.

Investigative problem

A fluent language-model answer cannot be told apart, on its surface, from one that is faithfully grounded in the documents. For research work, an unverifiable answer is worse than none. Nimillux treats claim verification as part of the architecture rather than a later check, and runs with no commercial inference API, which keeps the source corpus confidential.

Architecture and tools

  • Ingestion: PDFs, with OCR for scanned material, split by semantic chunking.
  • Retrieval: a FAISS vector index combined with BM25 lexical scoring and a maintained concept registry that resolves known aliases.
  • Generation: a locally hosted Mistral 7B model.
  • Verification: every claim is classified as strong, weak or unsupported by its best-aligned source passage, with an optional, architecturally independent secondary embedder.
  • Interface: multi-turn conversation, cross-document synthesis and a contradiction-detection mode.

Data and scale

A research corpus of tens of thousands of documents across technical and financial disciplines, sourced and curated by hand. The system runs on an 8 GB Apple M1 with the corpus on external disk. The corpus and source code are not public.

Engineering capabilities

  • Three-tier evidential confidence. An audit showed that the original binary supported/unsupported check was hiding a band of weakly supported claims. The redesign separates strong (similarity ≥ 0.65), weak (0.55–0.65) and unsupported claims.
  • Abstention. A pre-generation gate declines to answer when retrieval coverage is insufficient, rather than generating around irrelevant context.
  • Citation validation that catches fabricated reference identifiers.
  • Measured hardware envelope. Stress tests showed a 14B model silently truncating beyond 4,096 tokens and an 8B model running out of GPU memory above about 8,000 tokens, which set the limit at about 7B parameters and 4K context.

Testing and verification

A hand-labeled golden query suite grew from 6 to more than 27 entries during development. Each entry fixes the expected concepts, a minimum coverage and the expected routing domain, and no change is accepted until the full suite passes without regression. Ten categories of query-understanding failure were identified; nine are closed and protected by regression tests.

Limitations

  • The secondary verification pathway is implemented but not yet validated end to end, and is off by default.
  • One query-parsing failure category remains open.
  • Repeat-run stability of claim-level tiers under stochastic generation has not been characterized.
  • The tiers use fixed similarity cut points; they are not probabilistically calibrated.
  • The evaluation suite is small and hand-labeled.
